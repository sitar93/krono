# KRONO v1.5.0

This update adds a whole new tier of ten self-evolving modes, gives the MOD LED a lot more to say, makes tap tempo far more precise, and fixes how KRONO follows square-wave clocks. All of it runs on the existing hardware: no modifications needed.

## A little unexpected discovery: LFOs and audio

KRONO has no DAC and was never designed to produce analogue signals. While teaching the MOD LED to breathe, we realised the same technique could reach some of the outputs: by switching them millions of times per second, the existing output stage smooths the pulses into real, continuous voltages.

So outputs **1A**, **2A** and **2B** (and **3A** for modulation) can now produce smooth waveforms from slow LFOs up to audio rate, with a characterful lo-fi sound when used as oscillators. The signals are unipolar, from 0 to about 8 V.

## New DELTA tier (modes 31-40)

Hold **TAP** for about **4 seconds** (the MOD LED flashes 4 times), release, click **MOD** 1-10 times and confirm with **TAP**.

All DELTA modes evolve on their own over time, locked to the tempo. In every DELTA mode **1B** is the base clock and **3B** is a counter-phase clock (on the half beat). MOD selects the variant in the last column, and your choice is saved.

| Mode | Info | MOD |
|---|---|---|
| **31 TIDES** | 4 tempo-synced LFOs (1A: 1 beat, 2A: 4, 2B: 16, 3A: 2). The waveform slowly morphs into the next one. Gates on cycle starts and peaks | waveform |
| **32 ORBIT** | One sine at 4 phases (1A, 2A, 2B, 3A) rotating across the outputs. Direction reverses and speed drifts | rotation length |
| **33 CHIMERA** | Morphing wavetables (3A = morph position). The bank changes every 8 bars | bank |
| **34 WANDER** | Random voltages at different rates. Range and change probability evolve | smooth / stepped / slewed |
| **35 BUTTERFLY** | Lorenz attractor (x, y, z, speed). Drifts between calm and chaotic phases | speed |
| **36 MONOLITH** | Modal drone with a slow chord progression and very slow glides. Analog voices morph sine → saw → triangle and breathe; pulse voices with slow PWM swell in and out | progression on / frozen |
| **37 STATIC** | Noise and crackle that change colour and density | rain / wind / radio / geiger |
| **38 PULSAR** | Drum machine (analog kick, zap and tom, plus 1-bit hats, snare and blips). Patterns mutate, with fills | techno / broken / half-time / glitch |
| **39 ALLOY** | Two oscillators with sum, ring mod and logic combinations. The interval glides over time | evolution speed |
| **40 ASCENT** | Arpeggiator that mutates notes and progression, with rests and ratchets | scale |

In the audio modes (36-40) the pitch follows the tempo: change the tempo and the whole mode transposes. Patch the audio outputs into AC-coupled inputs (or attenuate them), as they are hot unipolar signals. Audio modes stay silent for 2 seconds after a mode change or a save, so switching is clean.

## The MOD LED now tells you more

The LED under MOD shows the state of the current mode:

- **Steady on**: a freeze, stop or "B" bank is active (for example A/B swap in modes 1-8, freeze in 16, 20 and 22, mute in 30).
- **Breathing**: the mode is continuously evolving or modulating; the stronger the modulation, the deeper the breath.
- **Repeating blinks**: the selected bank, pattern or profile number (for example the bank in mode 11 or the pattern in mode 23), repeated every 2 seconds.

It still flashes on taps, parameter changes and saves. In modes where it is steady on, the flash becomes a short off-blink so you can always see it.

**Mode selection is clearer too.** The Fast-Skip tiers now flash 1, 2, 3 and 4 times (ALPHA, BETA, OMEGA, DELTA). While selecting, the MOD LED lights with each MOD click, and on confirm both LEDs stay lit while the selection is saved.

The **status LED** (under TAP) still shows the active mode: short blinks for units, long blinks for tens. For example, mode 2 is 2 short blinks, mode 12 is 1 long and 2 short, mode 30 is 3 long, and mode 40 is 4 long.

## Precise tap tempo

Tap tempo has been rebuilt to follow you musically:

- **Tap 4 times** and the tempo locks, starting right on your 4th tap.
- **Keep tapping** and every tap refines the tempo, with no jumps in the rhythm. After 16-32 taps KRONO stays in time with the music for a long time, even after you stop.
- **Back in phase:** if it slowly drifts away, tap a few times along with the beat; it realigns smoothly, even after a long pause.
- **Mistakes are forgiven:** a stray tap is ignored and skipped beats are counted correctly.
- **Round tempos:** when your taps are very close to a whole BPM value (120, 128…), KRONO settles exactly on it.

### Optional: tap latency calibration

Most people tap slightly ahead of the beat they hear. KRONO can now measure your personal timing and compensate for it. Hold **MOD** while powering on until the two LEDs blink alternately, release, then tap 16 times along with the 100 BPM click on 1A. Patching 1A into a sound and tapping to what you hear works best. Both LEDs flash twice when it is done; press MOD to cancel. The result is kept across power cycles and future firmware updates. A new KRONO works perfectly well without it.

## Better external clock

- **Square-wave clocks are now followed on the beat.** KRONO used to react to the *end* of each incoming pulse: with short triggers nobody noticed, but with square-wave clocks the outputs ended up half a beat late. It now reacts to the start of the pulse, and the same fix applies to the MOD input, which now reacts as soon as a gate starts.
- When you unplug the clock, KRONO keeps both the tempo and the phase it was following, measured precisely over many beats, so it stays in time instead of drifting.
- Small timing differences between KRONO and a slightly slower master no longer cause occasional double hits.

## Updating

Use **`krono_code_v1.5.0.bin`** (or the `.hex`) with the [Jolin online updater](https://jolin.tech/krono_updater) or your preferred DFU tool. To enter the bootloader, hold **BOOT** and press **RESET**, as usual.

**Note:** this version stores more settings than before, so after updating, the saved tempo, mode and mode parameters go back to their defaults once.

**Firmware packaging** — build target PlatformIO `blackpill_f411ce`. The release includes the DFU-suffixed `.bin` (for the online updater), `.hex` and `.elf`; `*_raw.bin` is for STM32CubeProgrammer only.
